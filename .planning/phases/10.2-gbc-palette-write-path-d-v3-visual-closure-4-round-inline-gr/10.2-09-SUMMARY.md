## Scope-Shift Acknowledgment

The original phase title ("gbc-palette-write-path-d-v3-visual-closure") was a misnomer in light of the 7-probe bisect chain (Plans 03-06d):

- **The GBC palette WRITE path was never broken.** OCPD slot 2 contained the exact `cyan_pal` bytes in every probe, including the all-3-emissions-combined Probe B that visibly broke cyan.
- **The actual defect was a sprite-tile VRAM collision.** With LCDC.4=1, the BG and sprite tile regions share `$8000-$97FF`. Plan 22's hoisted `set_bkg_data(0, 1, _checkerboard_bg_pattern)` ran AFTER `set_sprite_data(0u, 48u, elephant_tiles)` in `main()`, overwriting elephant tile-0 bytes with checker bytes.
- **The fix is emission-order, not palette-write.** Plan 10.2-08 swapped `addAll(hoistedBgFillCheckerboardStatements)` before `addAll(allSpriteDataLoads)` in `buildMainFunction()`. Elephant sprite tiles win the shared-region write because they come last.

Future search should match "sprite tile VRAM collision (LCDC.4=1)" rather than "palette write path."
