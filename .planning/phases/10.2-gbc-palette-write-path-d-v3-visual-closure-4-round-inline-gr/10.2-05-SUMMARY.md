## Decisions Made

**Decision 1: Plan 22 is the regression cluster**
CYAN flips from YES (Probe A, Plans 19+20) to NO (Probe B, Plan 22). The 3 Plan 22 emissions together caused the regression. Plan 22 has 3 sub-emissions that need individual attribution — Plans 06a/06b/06c will test them independently.

**Decision 2: Scope-shift acknowledged**
The phase title "gbc-palette-write-path" is now misnamed in spirit. Evidence shows:
- BCPD slot 0 first-color = 0x7FFF (correct — `_gbkt_default_bg_pal` is loaded into palette RAM)
- OCPD slot 2 first-color = 0x7FFF (correct — cyan_pal IS in palette RAM)
- Sprite renders gray (0xA8A8A8 / 0x505050) — NOT cyan

The palette WRITE registers (BCPS/BCPD, OCPS/OCPD) are functioning correctly. The regression is in the sprite TILE data or OAM attribute byte path. Most likely candidate: **Emission #3** — bgFillCheckerboard hoist into main() runs AFTER `set_sprite_data(0u, 48u, elephant_tiles)`. Since LCDC.4=1 (shared sprite/BG $8000 tile region), `set_bkg_data(0, 1, ...)` OVERWRITES sprite tile 0 with checker bytes, corrupting the elephant sprite tile data. Alternatively, the OAM attribute byte `current_base_prop=2` may be reset to 0 somewhere in the new main() hoist sequence.

**Decision 3: Sub-narrow + acknowledge scope shift (user choice)**
Plans 06a/06b/06c will isolate the 3 Plan 22 emissions independently (each capturing its own V-5 triplet), providing per-emission visual evidence rather than inferring. Plan 07 finding doc will lead with the scope-shift framing.

The regression signal (CYAN=NO at Probe B) is unambiguous despite the frame-timing difference. Full byte-identical reproduction of the HEAD all-black state requires either a frame-61 capture or the sub-narrow (Plan 06) to isolate exactly which emission causes which visual artifact.
