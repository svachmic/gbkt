## Summary Narrative

Phase 10.2 successfully closes defect DEF-10.1-13-C (GBC screenshot renders grayscale/all-black despite JVM GREEN — escaped 4 inline fix rounds in Phase 10.1). The phase executed a rigorous 7-probe bisect chain that traced the regression to an interaction between two of Plan 10.1-22's emissions: `set_bkg_palette` (BCPD write) and `bgFillCheckerboard hoist` (BG tile data write). The root cause was a sprite-tile VRAM collision: with LCDC.4=1, both BG and sprite tiles share the `$8000-$97FF` region, and Plan 22's order caused checker bytes to overwrite elephant tile 0 after the sprite tiles were loaded.

The fix — two swapped `addAll()` calls in `GBDKPipelineV2.buildMainFunction()` (commit f2e8cecc) — is minimal, surgical, and locked by a new RED→GREEN test (`DV3VisualV3DiagnosticTest`). All 5 pre-existing codegen-shape tests remain GREEN. Both ROM builds pass clean. The behavior3 PNG (1325 bytes, 5 distinct colors) shows a cyan elephant on a checker background, confirming visual closure.

The phase also delivered SEED-012 MCP tooling (`emulator_read_memory` + `emulator_write_memory`) with CR-01 overflow guard and WR-01 range validation, both verified in source and covered by tests. The scratch/bisect worktree was torn down cleanly with zero probe-commit leakage onto `feat/d_and_d_gaps`.

**Phase title note:** The phase title "gbc-palette-write-path" is a misnomer — the bisect revealed the palette write path was never broken. The actual defect was a sprite-tile VRAM collision (LCDC.4=1 shared `$8000-$97FF` region). Title kept as-is for traceability per scope-shift acknowledgment in defect-closure-matrix.md.
