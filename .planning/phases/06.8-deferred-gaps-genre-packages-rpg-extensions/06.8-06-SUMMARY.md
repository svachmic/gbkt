## What Was Built

### Domain Types (PlatformerTypes.kt)

All platformer domain data classes and enums:

- **PlatformerPhysicsConfig**: gravity, jumpForce, terminalVelocity, coyoteFrames, jumpBufferFrames, airControlFactor, variableHeightJump, wallJump
- **WallJumpConfig**: wallSlideSpeed, iFrameDuration, cooldownFrames
- **PlatformType** enum: SOLID, ONE_WAY, MOVING, CRUMBLING
- **PlatformDef**: id, type, moveSpeed, crumbleDelay, crumbleRespawn
- **CameraScrollMode** enum: SMOOTH_FOLLOW, SCREEN_LOCK
- **ScrollDirection** enum: HORIZONTAL, VERTICAL, MULTI
- **PlatformerCameraConfig**: mode, deadZoneX, deadZoneY, scrollDirections, parallaxLayers
- **ParallaxLayer**: assetId, scrollSpeedX, scrollSpeedY
- **HazardDef**: id, tileId, damage, instant
- **LadderConfig**: climbSpeed, tileId
- **GoalZoneDef**: id, x, y, width, height
- **CollectibleType** enum: COIN, POWER_UP, CHECKPOINT, KEY
- **CollectibleDef**: id, type, value, tileId

### DSL Builders (PlatformerBuilders.kt)
