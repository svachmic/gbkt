## Issues Encountered
- The `gbkt-gradle-plugin` is an `includeBuild` that Gradle compiles eagerly before any `:gbkt-ir:*` tasks run. After adding the `io.github.gbkt.core.ir.SpriteMode` import to ConvertSpritesTask.kt, the plugin failed to compile because the published mavenLocal artifact predates the new enum. Required: publish gbkt-ir to mavenLocal first (with `-x :gbkt-gradle-plugin:compileKotlin` to break the circular dependency), then compile the plugin normally.
