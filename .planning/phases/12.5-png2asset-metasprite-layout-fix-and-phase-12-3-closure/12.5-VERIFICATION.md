### Audit Item (NOT a gap — planned fallback)

**REQ-3b mechanical pixel-diff gate: 8.78% (below 10% threshold)**

`VisualDiff.compare(walkFrame0.png, facingLeft.png, tolerance=0.10)` returned `match=true` (8.78% diff, below the 10% acceptance threshold). This outcome was explicitly planned for in Plan 09 Task 1 Step 3 and the RESEARCH document's Pitfall 5 fallback protocol:

- The plan states: "If VisualDiff returns `match=true` unexpectedly: REQ-3a (human-verify) is the PRIMARY closure signal per CLAUDE.md Visual Evidence Rule. Do NOT silently widen the tolerance."
- The 8.78% diff means the mechanical gate did NOT pass, but the human-verify gate (REQ-3a) IS the closure signal per the phase design.
- The `PlatformerTemplateUatTest.anchor4MetaspriteAnimation` test will FAIL on CI with tolerance=0.10 because 8.78% < 10%.

This is an acknowledged limitation documented in the debug session and Plan 09 SUMMARY. The phase goal's primary acceptance signal is the human-verify gate (REQ-3a), which passed. Phase closure is valid per the SPEC design.

If a tighter mechanical gate is desired going forward, a future plan should either:
(a) Reduce the VisualDiff threshold to ≤8.78% (accepting the measured diff as sufficient), or
(b) Extend the pre-scroll accumulation to push more background tilemap pixels into the diff.
