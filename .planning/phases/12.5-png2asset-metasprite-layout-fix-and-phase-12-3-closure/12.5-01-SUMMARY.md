(Game Boy ROM image, Rev.01, MBC5+RAM+BATT). No `make` invocation was needed.

client to `gbkt-mcp-server-0.1.0-SNAPSHOT-all.jar`). The emulator was stepped:

Both screenshots show a clearly recognizable duck/player character with head, body, and legs
visible — a proper 24x32 sprite as documented.
