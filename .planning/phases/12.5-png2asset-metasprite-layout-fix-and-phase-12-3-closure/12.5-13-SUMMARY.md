## What Was Built

Added `emits_actor_sprite_entries_in_sprites_array` as Test 7 in `GBDKPipelineV2MetadataSpritesTest`. The test:

1. Constructs a `GameIR` with one actor (`paddle`) that has `sprite = SpriteDef(assetRef = AssetRef("sprites/paddle.png"), size = SizeDef(8, 8))` and no metasprites.
2. Calls `pipeline.buildMetadataFile(fixture)`.
3. Asserts `sprites[]` has at least one entry for `spritePath="sprites/paddle.png"`.
4. Asserts `id="paddle"` (derived via `substringAfterLast('/').substringBeforeLast('.')`).
5. Asserts `includePath="sprites/paddle.h"` (derived as `spritePath` with `.h` extension).
6. Asserts `mirrorDedup=false` (actor sprites always false, no opt-in at actor level).
7. Asserts the 5 cutting-flag keys (`spriteMode`, `pivotX`, `pivotY`, `frameWidth`, `frameHeight`) are ABSENT from the entry — D-05 invariant guard.
