# Phase 12.5: png2asset-metasprite-layout-fix-and-phase-12-3-closure — Specification

**Created:** 2026-05-24
**Ambiguity score:** 0.178 (gate: ≤ 0.20)
**Requirements:** 7 locked

## Goal

The platformer-template player metasprite renders as visibly-recognizable duck art (not a malformed blob) in the post-12.5 ROM, with a horizontally-flipped left-facing frame distinguishable from the right-facing frame both mechanically (pixel-diff > 10%) AND on human visual inspection. Phase 12.3 anchor 4 closes GREEN as a side effect; the three deferred Phase 12.4 code-review warnings (WR-01/02/03) are also resolved.

## Background

Phase 12.4 closed PARTIAL on 2026-05-24 with 14/16 plans GREEN. Plan 12.4-15 (anchor 4 re-shoot) tripped human visual verification: the duck sprite renders as a small malformed blob on screen, not as a recognizable 24×32 duck. The 7.47% pixel-diff between facing-right and facing-left frames is real but spurious — it's between two broken renderings, not a working flip.

Investigation against the generated code traced the root cause to a layout disagreement:

- Generated `sprite_player_frame_0[]` is `{dy=-12,dx=-6,dtile=0}, {dy=8,dx=0,dtile=2}, {dy=8,dx=0,dtile=4}, {dy=-16,dx=16,dtile=6}, {dy=8,dx=0,dtile=8}, {dy=8,dx=0,dtile=10}, {metasprite_end}`.
- Tracing deltas places 6 sprites in a 2col × 3row arrangement (16 wide × 48 tall pixels with an 8-pixel column gap), NOT the 3col × 2row 24×32 layout that `-sw 24 -sh 32 -spr8x16` should produce.
- The gbkt codegen reproduces png2asset's emitted output byte-for-byte (verified by Plan 12.4-08's captured `evidence/png2asset-first-run/player_character_gbapduck_sprites.c`). The bug is therefore **upstream of the gbkt port** — in one of: (a) png2asset flag set, (b) source PNG's tile-grid layout, or (c) misunderstanding of png2asset's metasprite_t column/row ordering convention in SPR8x16 mode.

Phase 12.4 also landed the salvaged HOME bank overflow fix (`#pragma bank 1` injection in `ConvertSpritesTask`), without which the platformer-template ROM hangs at boot. Phase 12.5's visual-closure work depends on that fix already being in place.

The diagnostic anchor is the **reference GBDK-2020 platformer example** that gbkt's `gbkt-examples/platformer-template` was originally ported from. If the reference example renders the duck art correctly when its png2asset+lcc toolchain runs end-to-end, then the bug is gbkt's misconfiguration (workaround in scope). If even the reference example fails, png2asset has a real upstream bug (escalation — Phase 12.5 stops at "documented escalation" rather than patching the binary).

2. **Metasprite layout correction (gbkt-misconfig branch only)**: If REQ-1 verdict is `branch=gbkt-misconfig`, the `_player_metasprite_*` arrays generated for the platformer-template's player sprite arrange 6 hardware sprites into a 3col × 2row grid covering 24×32 pixels (not 2col × 3row covering 16×48px with an 8px gap as today).
   - Current: Generated `sprite_player_frame_0[]` lays out as 2col × 3row (16×48), with sprites at x={-6, +10} and y={-12, -4, +4}.
   - Target: Generated `sprite_player_frame_0[]` lays out as 3col × 2row (24×32), with sprites covering all 3 columns (x={-12, -4, +4}) and 2 rows (y={-16, 0}) — OR any geometrically-equivalent layout that produces the same on-screen 24×32 duck appearance as the reference GBDK example.
   - Acceptance: Tracing the cumulative deltas of `sprite_player_frame_0[]` in `gbkt-examples/platformer-template/build/gbkt/generated/main.c` yields a 3col × 2row covering pattern; OR an automated check (e.g., a JVM-tier metasprite-geometry test) asserts the cumulative absolute positions form 3 distinct x-columns within an 8-pixel-wide tolerance.

3. **Visible duck art (visual closure)**: The post-12.5 platformer-template ROM renders the player metasprite as recognizable duck art on both facing-right (facingRot=0) and facing-left (facingRot=3) frames.
   - Current: The post-12.4 ROM renders the player metasprite as a small malformed blob (per Plan 12.4-15's user visual verdict). 7.47% pixel-diff between facings is spurious noise.
   - Target: Fresh anchor4-walk-frame-0.png and anchor4-facing-left.png from the post-12.5 ROM both show recognizable duck art (head, body, legs visible enough that a human reviewer not pre-primed for the answer would identify "this is a duck/bird sprite" — not "this is a malformed blob").
   - Acceptance: BOTH gates must pass: (a) human visual inspection of the two fresh PNGs confirms recognizable duck art on each; (b) `VisualDiff.compare(walkFrame0.png, facingLeft.png, tolerance=0.10)` returns `match=false` (i.e., > 10% pixels differ — stricter than the 12.4-15 threshold of 5%).
