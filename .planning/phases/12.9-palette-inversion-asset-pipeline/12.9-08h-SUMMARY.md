## User question answered — the all-white 01 frame

The all-white `01-nextlevel-flip.png` is a **transient clear frame**, not a bug and not the card.
`nextLevelScene_enter()` (bank1.c:73-82) clears the BG via `fill_bkg_rect(0u,0u,32u,32u,0u)` (to wipe
the prior level's tiles — THIS is the D1 fix that removed the `0F`), then redraws the NEXT LEVEL card
centered. Single-stepped the transition: gameplay → card-at-top-left (frame 193) → blank clear
(frame 195) → centered card (frame 199) — ~12 frames (~0.2s) total, with the blank lasting ~1-3
frames. My single 08g screenshot happened to land on that clear frame. In play it is a brief flash;
the settled card is `02-nextlevel-card.png`.
