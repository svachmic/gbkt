## RC-1 fix (R-01 / R-05) — per-zone set_bkg_palette in setup_current_level

`GBDKPipelineV2.buildSetupCurrentLevelFunctionIfNeeded()` per-zone template now emits, right
after the existing `set_bkg_data(0u, _zone_<id>_tileset_count, _zone_<id>_tileset);` line:

```
set_bkg_palette(0u, _zone_<id>_tileset_PALETTE_COUNT, _zone_<id>_tileset_palettes);
```

Single template-string addition — no AST surgery, no header work (the `_PALETTE_COUNT` macro,
`_palettes` extern, and `<gb/cgb.h>` include are all provided by W4's `ConvertZoneTilesetsTask`).
Emitted unconditionally to mirror the existing `set_bkg_data` line and the main() startup
`_gbkt_default_bg_pal` upload — `cgb_compatibility()` makes `set_bkg_palette` a no-op on DMG
(RESEARCH RC-1d), so DMG safety is preserved without `#ifdef` noise.

This closes the W5 SceneVisitor blind spot (Pitfall 7): gameplay zones loaded through
`setup_current_level()` (empty `scene.zoneRefs`) now upload their authored palette instead of
inheriting the title scene's BG palette RAM.
