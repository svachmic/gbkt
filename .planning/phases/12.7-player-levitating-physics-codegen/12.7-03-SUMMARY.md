produces a Kotlin compile error when Plan 04 references `posYSym` inside the
function body. By landing the signature change in its own commit:

2. If Plan 04 needs to be reverted in isolation, the signature edit stays
   (and is harmless — the parameter is just unused until Plan 04 ships).
3. Reviewer can sign off on this 4-line diff in seconds.
