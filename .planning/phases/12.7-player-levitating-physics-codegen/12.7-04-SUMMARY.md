## D-02 Snap Formula (CONTEXT § decisions)

```text
foot_tile_row = (player_real_y + height) >> 3
posYSym       = ((foot_tile_row << 3) - height) << 4
```

Driven by the recorded inner `anyHit` IF condition firing — i.e., the foot probe hit a solid tile this frame. The single-assignment snap pins the player's foot-row to the top edge of the underlying solid tile, in one frame, deterministically. This is STRONGER than the reference `player.c`, which relies on the iterative `stuck-resolve` while-loop (Plan 05's scope) to converge on the same answer over multiple frames.

## Underflow-Clamp Decision (per Plan 01 diagnostic)

`.planning/phases/12.7-player-levitating-physics-codegen/evidence/diagnostic-baseline.md` § Section 2 captured the underflow analysis:

- The snap underflows when `(foot_tile_row << 3) < height`, i.e., `foot_tile_row < height / 8 = 3`.
- On platformer-template (height=24), the floor tile-rows live at tile-row 17 (pixel 136) or below. The player can never land on a tile-row above row 3 — no level data contains a solid tile in rows 0–2 of any zone.
- **Verdict (per CONTEXT § Claude's Discretion):** UNREACHABLE on platformer-template geometry. No `max(0, ...)` clamp emitted in this plan.
- A single-line `CComment` after the snap documents the boundary: `"foot_tile_row >= height/8 verified non-negative for current spawn/height geometry — Plan 01 diag."`
