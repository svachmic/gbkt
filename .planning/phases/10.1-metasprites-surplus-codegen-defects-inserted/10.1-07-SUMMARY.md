## Decisions Made

- **`List<String>` over `List<CRawCode>` for `metaspriteAutoExterns`.** The Plan's interface delta said `CRawCode(...)`, but `CFile.rawSections: List<String>` (verified at `gbkt-backend-gbdk/.../codegen/ast/CFile.kt:44`) and existing entries `paletteExternRaw` / `callOpForwardDecls` are both `String?`. The Plan's Task 2 `<action>` explicitly anticipated and authorized this divergence ("If `CRawCode` is not the right type for header raw sections...use the matching type. Existing `paletteExternRaw` and `callOpForwardDecls` types..."). Not a deviation — a plan-anticipated type-discipline correction at the read-first step.
- **Single-scene test shape, not 2-scene.** Plan 06's `Seed009BankIncludeTest` had to construct a 2-scene game to escape `BankingAnalysisPass`'s single-scene HOME fast-path because it tested `bank1.c` shape. WR-02 tests `game.h` content, which is bank-0 (always emitted regardless of routing). A single trivial scene (`SceneIR(id = "title")`) is sufficient; no Pitfall 2 (fast-path silently passing) risk.
- **Pre-existing Seed004 RED logged in commit body and SUMMARY, NOT in `deferred-items.md`.** `Seed004ElephantTileRenderingDiagnosticTest` is the *named-root-cause anchor* introduced by Plan 10.1-10 commit `da9fa139` to drive Plan 10.1-11's D-V1 Edit 2 fix in `buildMainFunction`. It is a tracked sibling-plan RED, not a discovered issue. Per SCOPE BOUNDARY: pre-existing failures in unrelated files are out of scope for this plan; structurally unrelated (reads `main.c`, asserts `SPRITES_8x8;` ordering vs WR-02's `game.h` extern decl).
