<objective>
Per D-05 — third diagnostic-plan iteration for D-V3. Plans 10.1-19 + 10.1-20 closed the bootstrap-order layer (DV3GbcPaletteWriteDiagnosticTest 3/3 GREEN, codegen-shape reference-aligned), but post-Wave-8 user-verified visual re-shoot shows GBC screen STILL all-black. The bootstrap-order hypothesis was necessary but insufficient — there's a 4th layer we haven't named.

Three new candidate hypotheses to test:

1. **cgb_compatibility puts GBC into DMG-compat mode.** In DMG-compat mode, set_sprite_palette may be a no-op or write to a non-effective register set. The reference may either skip cgb_compatibility OR pair it with set_default_palette / direct BCPD/OCPD writes.
2. **OAM attribute byte hardcoded to S_PAL(0).** The metasprite descriptor table emits per-tile attribute bytes; on GBC, bits 0-2 select sprite palette slot. If gbkt's MetaspriteVisitor hardcodes S_PAL(0), the _elephant_subPalette variable cycles uselessly because no OAM entry ever references slots 1/2/3.
3. **Descriptor table needs per-frame regeneration.** Reference's `metasprite_t` table may dynamically rebuild OAM attribute bytes from current _subPalette each frame; gbkt may emit the table once at codegen-time with fixed attribute bytes.

Plan 10.1-22 fixes whichever is the named cause.
</objective>
