HYPOTHESIS #2 CONFIRMED (port-side runtime mode): port's main.c does NOT emit either
SPRITES_8x8 or SPRITES_8x16 between cgb_compatibility() and DISPLAY_ON. GBDK default
after cgb_compatibility() leaves the sprite hardware in 8x16 mode.

HYPOTHESIS #1 ALSO ACTIVE (asset-pipeline side): port's ConvertSpritesTask runs
png2asset with -spr8x16 for the elephant (because PNG height 240 >= 16). This causes
the tile-data layout to differ from the reference's -spr8x8 layout. The port's
elephant_tiles[896] interleaves blanks (top+bottom-of-tile pairs) where the reference's
sprite_tiles[768] is dense 8x8 tile data. The DSL's tile(...) indices in Metasprites.kt
were transcribed from the reference's -spr8x8 numbering, so they reference half-tiles
in the port's -spr8x16 layout.

Named root cause (singular per D-05): **sprite-mode mismatch across the gbkt pipeline**.
The asset pipeline (ConvertSpritesTask) and the runtime emission (GBDKPipelineV2's
main) do not agree on 8x8 vs 8x16, AND neither matches the user's DSL transcription
intent (8x8).

Fix shape for Plan 11 (see sprite-mode-init-finding.md):
  - Force ConvertSpritesTask to emit -spr8x8 for assets used by metasprites (the
    natural primitive for metasprites is 8x8); AND
  - Emit `SPRITES_8x8;` in main.c between cgb_compatibility() and DISPLAY_ON when the
    GameIR contains at least one metasprite.

Both edits are required because each, alone, leaves the other layer mismatched.
