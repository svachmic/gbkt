## Accomplishments

- **MetaspriteIR.kt:** 4 new nullable `String?` var-ref-name fields (`posXVarName`, `posYVarName`, `idxVarName`, `rotVarName`) inserted BEFORE `sourceLocation` so the historical positional constructor `MetaspriteIR(id, frames, sourceLocation = …)` keeps working. Comprehensive KDoc explains the visitor-side null-fallback contract (visitor substitutes `_posX`/`_posY`/`_idx`/`_rot` canonical globals when null).
- **ScriptOp.kt MoveMetasprite:** 4 new nullable `String?` fields (`posXVar`, `posYVar`, `idxVar`, `rotVar`) with matching KDoc and null-fallback contract. The `accept()` method body and `ScriptOpVisitorI.visitMoveMetasprite(...)` signature are unchanged — the visitor walks the new fields directly via the `op` parameter per PATTERNS.md §Pattern Assignments line 212.
- **MetaspriteBuilder.kt:** 4 binder methods (`posX(varRef: AssignableVar)`, `posY(...)`, `idx(...)`, `rot(...)`) that capture `varRef.name` into `internal var` holders. The `build()` method propagates all 4 names into the constructed `MetaspriteIR`. The `moveMetasprite(ref)` DSL helper looks up the already-registered `MetaspriteIR` via `GameBuilderContext.current.findMetasprite(ref.id)` and mirrors the 4 names onto the emitted `MoveMetasprite` ScriptOp.
- **GameBuilder.kt:** Added `internal fun findMetasprite(id: String): MetaspriteIR?` — the read-side accessor for the `moveMetasprite()` helper to mirror the captured names without exposing the private `_metaspriteIRs` list.
- **Test coverage:** 7 new tests across 2 files — 2 in `:gbkt-ir` (field-default invariants), 5 in `:gbkt-lang` (full DSL propagation, back-compat absence-of-binders, independent posY/rot binders, all-four-together, 2-metasprites-distinct-vars stress preview for CR-03).

## Decisions Made

- **Test split across modules (planning-anticipated):** Test 3 from the plan's MetaspriteIRVarRefFieldsTest (DSL propagation) lives in `:gbkt-lang/MetaspriteBuilderVarRefFieldsTest.kt` (not `:gbkt-ir`) because `gbkt-ir` is the leaf module with zero gbkt dependencies and cannot reach the DSL surface (`game()`, `metasprite()`, `i16Var()`). The plan acceptance criteria explicitly authorized this split ("2 in IR module + 1 in lang module per the test-placement note"). I went further and added 4 additional `:gbkt-lang` tests to cover back-compat, independent binders, all-four-together, and the 2-metasprites stress preview — these strengthen the substrate-for-CR-03 guarantee without expanding scope.
- **Field order in MetaspriteIR:** New fields inserted BEFORE `sourceLocation` (id, frames, [4 new], sourceLocation). Rationale: 7 existing call sites in `MetaspriteIRTest.kt` and `GameIRSerializer.kt:317-324` use the form `MetaspriteIR(id, frames, sourceLocation = loc)` — by placing the new fields before `sourceLocation` and giving them defaults, the named-arg call sites resolve unchanged and the positional `MetaspriteIR("x", listOf(frame))` calls take the defaults. Zero call-site churn outside this plan.
- **Mirror-from-IR pattern in moveMetasprite():** The helper reads the already-registered `MetaspriteIR` (registered by `MetaspriteDelegate.provideDelegate` BEFORE the scene's `frame { }` block runs) and copies the 4 names onto the emitted `MoveMetasprite` ScriptOp. Rationale: (a) keeps the Plan 05 visitor stateless w.r.t. the metasprite registry; (b) locks the IR/ScriptOp-name snapshot at script-build time so a future re-binding could not silently desync the two; (c) avoids the visitor needing access to the full game registry to look up the IR by id at codegen time.
