## Decisions Made
- **PoolGetActiveCount delegates to CallExpr**: New `Expr` subtype overrides `accept()` to delegate to `CallExpr("pool_${poolId}_active_count", emptyList())` — avoids adding a new method to `ExprVisitorI`, keeping all existing visitors working without changes.
- **maxSize embedded in ScriptOps at DSL time**: `ScriptOpVisitor` is a singleton object with no access to `GameIR`. `PoolForEachActive` and `PoolDestroyAll` carry `maxSize: Int` filled from `ActorPoolRef.maxSize` when the DSL method is called.
- **Death callback via ScriptBuilderContext.with()**: Follows the established codebase pattern (used in other nested DSL builders) rather than exposing private `ops` field.
