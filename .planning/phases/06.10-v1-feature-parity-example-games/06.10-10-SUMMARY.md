### DungeonGameTest.kt (8 tests)

SimulationContextV2 scenario tests:

2. keys starts at 0 on gameplay entry

5. Torch decrements when steps=4 (multiple of 4 > 0 condition fires)
6. Title scene enters correctly and has print ops
7. Gameover scene enters correctly and has print ops
