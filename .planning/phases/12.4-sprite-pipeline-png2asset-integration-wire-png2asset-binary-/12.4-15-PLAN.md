<objective>
Re-shoot the Phase 12.3 anchor 4 UAT with the post-Phase-12.4 ROM, capturing fresh PNGs that show
visible duck art with a pixel-diff > 5% between facing-right (facingRot=0) and facing-left (facingRot=3)
frames. Covers SPEC REQ-6 acceptance ("Pixel diff > 5% in metasprite-occupied region") + CONTEXT D-06
Plan N-1 (anchor 4 visual re-shoot).

Purpose: This is the visible-hflip closure — the symptom that started Phase 12.4. Phase 12.3-15
RED-shot anchor 4 with the pre-Phase-12.4 ROM and surfaced "facing-left looks identical to facing-right
because the player metasprite is a 64-byte checkerboard". After Plan 12.4-09's multi-tile composition
+ Plan 12.4-13's :buildRom smoke, the ROM should now render the duck art. This plan captures the
proof-of-fix PNGs + locks the pixel-diff assertion as a regression guard.

Per RESEARCH § Scout Target 8c, `VisualDiff.compare()` already exists at `gbkt-emulator/.../VisualDiff.kt:58` —
no new helper class needed. Two viable approaches: full-frame diff (simpler) or metasprite-bbox region crop
(more SPEC-precise). Plan adopts the full-frame approach with rationale: the player metasprite is ~24×32 px
in a 160×144 frame, so the metasprite occupies ~3.3% of the frame; an hflip producing > 5% full-frame
pixel-diff is a STRONG signal that the visible art is materially different (background tilemap is unchanged
between the two captures, so the diff is dominated by the metasprite region anyway).

Per the Visual Evidence Rule (CLAUDE.md): variable assertions are necessary but NOT sufficient for visual
truths. Fresh PNGs + pixel-diff are the required visual evidence.

Output: 2 fresh PNGs + 1 markdown diff report + 1 added test assertion.
</objective>
