Per RESEARCH Scout Target 7c:
- `-sw 24 -sh 32` = 24×32 pixel sprite frame = 3 columns × 4 rows of 8×8 tiles
- In `-spr8x16` mode (pairs of 8×8 tiles → one 8×16 tile), 3 columns × 2 8x16-tile-pairs = 6 `metasprite_t` entries per frame
- 6 animation frames × 6 entries = ~36 `tile()` DSL calls in total for the `player` metasprite (this plan does not author them; it captures the data Plan 12.4-09 needs)

PNG asset: `gbkt-examples/platformer-template/res/graphics/player-character-gbapduck-sprites.png`

- 12-frame atlas (per CONTEXT D-04 / README); only 6 used for the player metasprite (idle + 3 walk + 2 jump)
