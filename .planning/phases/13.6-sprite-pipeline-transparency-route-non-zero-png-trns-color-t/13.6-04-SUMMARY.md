## Decisions Made
- **SpritesExtension mirrors OptimizationExtension exactly** — abstract class, @Inject constructor, `objects.newInstance`, `fun sprites(action: Action<SpritesExtension>)`. No magic strings (Project Rule #1).
- **Strict gate fires first, overflow second** — strict short-circuits BEFORE overflow count (which is O(pixels)); overflow fires BEFORE auto-correct/permute and BEFORE exec. Both fire only when `transparentIdx > 0`.
- **Overflow counts USED pixels, not declared palette slots** — `countUsedVisibleColors(pngFile, transparentIdx)` counts non-transparent entries with at least one pixel; declared-but-unused slots are not counted (REQ-5 / D-05).
- **Tests 13/15 use real elephant.png** — `writeIndexedPngWithTrns` synthetic fixtures have a known ImageIO round-trip bug: after `ImageIO.write + ImageIO.read`, Java's PNG decoder reports `transparentPixel=0` regardless of the original index. Real elephant.png (verified tRNS=4 by `getTransparentIndexShared`) avoids this.

## Issues Encountered
- **ImageIO round-trip resets transparentPixel to 0**: Java's `IndexColorModel` + `ImageIO.write` + `ImageIO.read` for indexed PNG does not preserve a custom `transparentPixel` index. After round-trip, `transparentPixel=0` regardless of the original value. This means `writeIndexedPngWithTrns` fixtures cannot produce PNGs where `getTransparentIndexShared` reliably returns a non-zero index. Resolved by using the real `elephant.png` (proper PNG author-side tRNS chunk is preserved through `ImageIO.read` since it's a real indexed PNG, not a Java-synthesized one).
