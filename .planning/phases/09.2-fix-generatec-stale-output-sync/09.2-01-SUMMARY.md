- **Files modified:** `OutputDirSyncTest.kt`
- **Commit:** `aaf8f809` (fixed before commit)
- **Plan spec impact:** Plan acceptance criteria says "Each of the 6 backtick test names from <behavior> appears verbatim in the file" - the em-dash/dot names from the plan are not compilable Kotlin identifiers. The test semantics are preserved exactly.
