## Decisions Made

- **Threshold = 14_336 bytes** — fixed per SPEC Constraint (16 KB bank minus 2 KB safety margin); future per-zone banking work tracked by `SEED-PHASE-12-PER-ZONE-TILEMAP-BANKS` would obsolete this single-threshold model.
- **Compute overflow ONCE at run() entry** — both the single-scene fast-path and the bin-packer path consult the same `tilemapOverflow` value. This satisfies detekt's 4-return limit while still applying the guard to every successful exit.
- **Silently skip unresolvable PNGs** — overflow is a budget hint; missing-file diagnostics flow through `ConvertZoneTilesetsTask` (REQ-4).
- **Gradle plugin's `GenerateCTask` does NOT yet pass a real `assetRoot`** — out of scope per terminal-subphase rule. The guard is fully exercised by JVM tests that wire `assetRoot` directly; future Gradle-plugin wiring is a small change (one named argument).
