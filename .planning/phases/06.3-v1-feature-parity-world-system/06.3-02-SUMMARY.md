## Decisions Made

- `zone_transition` sequence: onExit dispatch (switch on `_current_zone_id`) → edge-based position mapping (switch on `edge`) → `zone_load` call — matches Gap 8 requirement (exit fires before load)
- 0xFF sentinel for `_current_zone_id` — no zone loaded yet; prevents false onExit dispatch on very first transition
- `buildZoneData()` returns `Pair<List<CVarDecl>, List<CDefine>>` — zone arrays are `const` data in main.c, not extern-declared in game.h; simpler than splitting into two separate injection points
- Tileset reuse guard compares `_current_tileset_id` before `set_bkg_data()` — avoids redundant VRAM writes for same-tileset zone transitions
