anchor 1 (`screenshotPath.length() > 100`) and anchor 2
(`screenshotPath.length() > 200`) with a perceptual non-uniform-pixel check

(uniform colour) MUST FAIL the check; a real-content PNG MUST PASS.

PNG (the current pre-11.2 blank) from a real-content PNG. The threshold passes
for both. Per `feedback_visual_evidence_for_visual_truths.md` and CLAUDE.md

re-shoot will produce real-content PNGs — but Plan 15 (the re-shoot) MUST run
AGAINST this hardened check, not against the weak byte-length threshold, to
prove the captured PNG is genuinely non-blank.

counts distinct RGB values (or non-uniform-pixel count, see <behavior> for
exact metric). The check decodes the PNG via `javax.imageio.ImageIO.read`,
iterates pixels, and asserts at least N distinct RGB values OR at least M%
non-background pixels.

This plan BLOCKS Plan 15 (Anchor 1+2 re-shoot): the re-shoot's PASS is the
proof the perceptual check is correctly tuned, and the re-shoot's PASS must
rest on this check, not on the byte-length threshold.
