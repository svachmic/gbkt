 *      slots populated by step 1; ordering is load-bearing (D-claude-4).
 *   3. `SHOW_BKG;` — re-enable BG layer (set_bkg_tiles disables it internally).

 * tile data does not flow through png2asset skip the set_bkg_data prepend.

 * the consuming bank file's `#include "_zone_<id>_tileset.h"` directive.
