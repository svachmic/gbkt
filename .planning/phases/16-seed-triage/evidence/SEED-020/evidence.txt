## Analysis

The substrate test suite is GREEN for GameIRSerializer because the 15 passing tests
test scenes, actors, variables, ScriptOps, etc. — none of them exercise the 10 stubbed
collections (zones, systems, flags, items, etc.).

The source shows all 10 SEED-020 stubs still present as emptyList() with explicit
SEED-020 marker comments. Any GameIR with zones/systems/flags/items/etc. would lose those
collections on round-trip — e.g., a zone-based RPG game serialized and deserialized would
come back with no zones.
