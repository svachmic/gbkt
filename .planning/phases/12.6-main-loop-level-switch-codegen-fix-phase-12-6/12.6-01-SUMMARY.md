## Decisions Made

1. **spawn(40u, 120u) LOCKED for Plan 12.6-07.** Reference uses `(40, 40)` hardcoded for all levels, BUT Y=40 relies on a fall-onto-floor startup pattern gbkt does not yet replicate. Y=120 places the player on the visible ground row, avoiding one-frame-in-the-air appearance and fall-through risk. Documented in `reference-toolchain-notes.md` § "SetupPlayer() spawn values" so future readers see the rationale before "fixing" Y back to 40.
2. **Subpixel <<4 + velocity-reset contracts codified** for Plan 12.6-05's per-zone case-body emission. Non-optional per Pitfalls 2/3 in 12.6-RESEARCH.md.
3. **Pre-fix manifest re-captured from scratch** (no Plan 12-26 D-overfitting-1 manifest existed under `.planning/phases/12-.../evidence/`; default plan behavior was re-capture, applied here).
4. **No source code changes this plan.** Pure evidence capture, per CONTEXT.
