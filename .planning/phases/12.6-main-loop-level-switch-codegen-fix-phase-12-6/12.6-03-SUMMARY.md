# Phase 12.6 Plan 03: ZoneIR Spawn Fields + ZoneBuilder.spawn() DSL Summary

Additive non-breaking IR + DSL surface for per-zone player spawn coordinates that
Plan 12.6-05 codegen will consume to emit per-level spawn tables, closing DEFECT-2
(D-06 / D-07): the framework currently has no DSL surface to declare a per-zone
player spawn, so `setup_current_level()` cannot route the player to the correct
position when a new level becomes active.

## Resume Signal

DSL → IR contract for `spawn()` locked. Plan 12.6-05 (codegen consumer) may now
proceed (waits on Plan 12.6-02 to also be merged for GBDKPipelineV2 file-overlap
reasons). The locked contract surface is:

- IR: `ZoneIR(spawnX: UByte? = null, spawnY: UByte? = null, ...)` — nullable
  defaults to preserve existing call sites; nullable encodes the "user did not
  declare a spawn" sentinel that Plan 12.6-05's build-time WARNING will key on.
- DSL: `ZoneBuilder.spawn(x: UByte, y: UByte)` — coordinates in pixels; codegen
  applies `<<4` shift to subpixel form.
- DSL ergonomics: numeric UByte coordinates only — no magic strings (Project
  Rule #1 / `feedback_no_magic_strings.md`).
