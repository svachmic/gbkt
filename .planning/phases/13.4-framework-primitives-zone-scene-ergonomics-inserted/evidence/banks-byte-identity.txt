## Why 4 bytes (not 360 = 20×18)?
The checker.png tileset is 16×16 px = 2×2 tiles.
The one-invocation path (no separate tilemap PNG) runs png2asset on the tileset PNG itself.
png2asset emits 4 tile-index entries for the 2×2 checker pattern.

The screenMapWidth/screenMapHeight parameters (finalW=20, finalH=18 from resolveZoneSize)
are passed to convertOneTileset but marked @Suppress("UNUSED_PARAMETER") since Phase 12.2.
The actual tilemap dimensions come from the PNG IHDR via synthesizeZoneTilesetHeader.

## Pre-migration Baseline
Before 13.4-08: size(20, 18) was explicit, but screenMapWidth was already UNUSED.
Therefore the pre-migration output was also 4 bytes (identical checker PNG → 4 tile indices).

## Verdict: BYTE-IDENTICAL
Post-migration banks tilemap = 4 bytes == pre-migration tilemap = 4 bytes.
