<objective>
Replace the dangerous magic `size(32, 32)` zone default (Req #14 / D-01..D-03) with a
deterministic PNG-derived → 20×18-fallback chain. `gbkt-lang` stays filesystem-free:
`ZoneBuilder.build()` emits a null "auto" sentinel for `mapWidth`/`mapHeight` when `size()`
is omitted; a PURE `resolveZoneSize(explicit?, derivedPngTileDims?)` holds the fallback
policy (tested in gbkt-lang); `ConvertZoneTilesetsTask` reads the tilemap PNG and back-fills.

Purpose: Eliminate the silent level-truncation bug (32×32 over a smaller tile array) and
the `size(20,18)` ceremony, while keeping the PNG read in the build-tool layer (D-01).
Output: `resolveZoneSize` + null sentinel + metadata/back-fill plumbing + ZoneSizeResolutionTest.
</objective>
