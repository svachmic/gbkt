#### 1. D-11 Gameplay Visual Parity Re-Confirmation

**Test:** Compare `evidence/pre-migration/gameplay.png` against `evidence/post-migration/gameplay.png` and confirm the rendering difference is acceptable.

**Expected:** The migrated platformer-template gameplay scene renders identically to the pre-migration baseline OR the difference is explained and accepted (e.g., emulator GBC mode variance, scroll position difference, known palette behavior).

**Why human:** The verifier observes a visible color-scheme difference between the two screenshots. Pre-migration gameplay shows the player sprite in a green/teal tone (consistent with GBC CGB palette mode). Post-migration gameplay shows the player in grey/white with green-colored ground tiles. The Plan-08 SUMMARY records the user approved this at the checkpoint:human-verify gate, noting "colors NOT inverted (green palette preserved — D-13: no revert of 13.3-22/24 or 12.9 keep_palette_order)." The SUMMARY's traversal recipe also notes slightly different game-state positions between captures (map_pos_x tracking). However, the verifier cannot programmatically distinguish between:

- (a) An acceptable rendering difference due to different emulator GBC color-correction mode between the two capture sessions, or different player positions in the world (the player character itself may look different depending on which animation frame / position was captured)
- (b) A real visual regression that slipped through the approval

The pre-migration and post-migration screenshots of the title screen ("GBDK-2020 PLATFORMER TEMPLATE") are visually identical. The NextLevel card ("NEXT LEVEL" centered banner) is visually byte-for-byte identical (749 bytes both). Only the gameplay screenshot differs in color tone.

**RESOLUTION (2026-06-05):** Root-caused to a capture-mode inconsistency, NOT a regression. The original
pre-migration title/gameplay (plan 04) were captured in **DMG mode** (gbcMode omitted in the first MCP
session); post-migration (plan 08) was captured in **GBC mode** — hence the green-DMG-tint vs GBC-palette
difference, with identical layout. At the user's direction the pre-migration title + gameplay were
re-captured in GBC mode against a temporarily-rebuilt pre-migration ROM, then the migrated state restored.
Result: GBC-mode **gameplay.png is BYTE-IDENTICAL** pre-vs-post (and nextlevel.png), and title.png is
visually identical with an expected byte diff from the intentional `by zone`→`screen()` migration. Zero
palette/render regression. Truth #12 → VERIFIED; phase status → **passed (12/12)**.
