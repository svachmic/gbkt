<objective>
Add the reusable settle-before-capture primitive to `StepAgent` and prove it with a stub-driven RED→GREEN JVM regression test in the `gbkt-emulator` suite.

Purpose: Immediately after a scene transition the PPU/VRAM has not latched the new scene, so `captureScreenshot()` reads a stale/blank frame (the 413-byte blank PNGs from 11.1-08; the reason 12.6 fell back to direct-MCP capture). A settle primitive that waits for frame-buffer stability before reading eliminates this failure class for automated regression. (Covers R-01 unit half + R-02.)

Output: `StepAgent.settle(): IntArray` + `StepAgent.captureScreenshotSettled(label): File`, and a new `SettleCaptureTest.kt` that fails without `settle()` and passes with it.
</objective>
