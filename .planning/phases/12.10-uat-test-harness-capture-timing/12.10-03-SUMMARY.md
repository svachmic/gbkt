## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 — Blocking] walkFrameIdx cycling assertion broke under the settle path**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (first run after routing captures through `captureScreenshotSettled`).
- **Issue:** The plan said to "preserve the existing walkFrameIdx variable assertions". But `captureScreenshotSettled` advances a fixed number of frames (settle loop) between captures, and that offset is a multiple of the walk-cadence period — so sampling walkFrameIdx once per settled capture landed on the SAME cadence phase every time (`[1,1,1]`, then `[0,0,0]` after moving the read post-settle). The original once-per-capture sampling could no longer observe cycling.
- **Fix:** Re-expressed the cycling assertion as a frame-by-frame scan — step RIGHT one frame at a time across the walk segments and collect the distinct walkFrameIdx values into a set, asserting `>= 2 distinct`. This preserves the corroborating "cadence cycled" intent (D-07 acceptance "preserve the variable assertions") but is independent of the settle phase. Also moved `facingRot` to a post-settle `readVariable` read so the asserted value reflects the captured frame.
- **Files modified:** PlatformerTemplateUatTest.kt
- **Commit:** `bbc9db66`

This is the natural realization of the plan's "preserve the variable assertions" instruction under the (intended) settle-capture timing change — the signal is preserved, only the sampling method is made settle-robust.
