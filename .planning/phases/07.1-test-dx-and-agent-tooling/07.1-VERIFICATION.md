## Anti-Patterns Found

No blockers or stubs found. The `return null` patterns in `GameDiscovery.configForGame()` and `GbktTestExtension.resolveConfig()` are intentional — they signal "ROM not yet built" and trigger JUnit5 assumption failures (test skips), not test failures. This is by design.
