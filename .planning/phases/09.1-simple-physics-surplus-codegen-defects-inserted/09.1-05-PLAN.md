<objective>
Pipeline-layer companion to Plan 04. Plan 04 added the analysis-layer fast-path that produces `BankSlot(bank = 0)` for single-scene-fits-HOME games. This plan consumes that signal in GBDKPipelineV2.buildSceneFile() so the compiled ROM for simple_physics is genuinely MBC 0x00 — byte 0x0147 reads `00` instead of `19`.

The structural change: when every scene in the annotated GameIR has `bankSlot?.bank == 0` (the Plan 04 fast-path outcome), the pipeline either suppresses bank1.c emission entirely OR emits an empty-shell CFile that contains no `#pragma bank 1` directive AND no BANKED functions. Scene functions (play_enter, play_frame, play_exit) are folded into main.c instead, where they execute in HOME without bank switching.

Because the change touches the pipeline emission contract, this plan also carries the user-visible compiled-ROM byte witness (Task 3's build-time od probe) AND the multi-example buildRom smoke regression (D-04 — banked games must NOT silently downgrade to ROM_ONLY just because of this fix).

This is Plan 2 of 2 for the gap closure. Plan 04 delivers the analysis decision; Plan 05 honors it at the emission layer and assembles the user-visible artifact (a 32 KB ROM_ONLY simple-physics.gb).
