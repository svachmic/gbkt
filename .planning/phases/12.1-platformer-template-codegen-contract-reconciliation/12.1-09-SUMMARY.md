## Why banks shows 4 diffs but RESEARCH predicted 2

RESEARCH §regression-sensitivity table predicted exactly 2 changed files for banks (`_zone_play_zone_tilemap.c` + `_zone_play_zone_tileset.h`). The actual sweep found 4, but the additional 2 (`game_metadata.json` + `main.c`) are not unexplained regressions — they are the **upstream metadata source** and **downstream header consumer** of the predicted 2:

- `game_metadata.json`'s `"bank": 2` field is the metadata that Plan 12.1-01 Task 2 reads to emit the `#pragma bank 2` on `_zone_play_zone_tilemap.c`. Plan 12.1-01-SUMMARY explicitly lists `buildMetadataFile` modifications in §Key-Files-Modified.
- `main.c`'s `#include "_zone_play_zone_tileset.h"` is the header-visibility prerequisite that makes Plan 12.1-03's `_zone_play_zone_tilemap_WIDTH/_HEIGHT` defines reach `setup_current_level`. Plan 12.1-03-SUMMARY explicitly documents this as a Rule 3 auto-fix deviation ("Without this the new preprocessor macros are never visible to setup_current_level").

The RESEARCH table was scoped to the **emitted user-facing C** (pragma + defines); the metadata and include wiring are the **plumbing** that makes those emissions reachable. Both are in-scope per the respective plan SUMMARYs.

## Methodology Note: `:generateC` vs `:buildRom` baseline parity

An initial baseline pass used `:clean :generateC` (cheaper, no GBDK toolchain invocation), but this produced a spurious "sprites/ subdirectory only in current" diff across pong / metasprites / metasprites-stress. Investigation revealed that the per-ROM `sprites/` directory (e.g., `pong/build/gbkt/generated/sprites/{ball,paddle}.{c,h}`) is populated only by `:buildRom`'s asset-pipeline path, not by `:generateC` alone. The sweep was re-run with `:clean :buildRom` baselines for apples-to-apples comparison. Final baselines live at `/tmp/12.1-09-baseline-buildRom/` (volatile; not committed). This methodology adjustment is recorded so the next sweep starts with `:buildRom` from the outset.
