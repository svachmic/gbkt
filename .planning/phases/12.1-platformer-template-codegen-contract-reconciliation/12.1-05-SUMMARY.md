## Architectural placement decision for TilemapCollisionBuilder

The plan's `<action>` left the choice open:
- Option A: keep builder in `PlatformerExtensions.kt` alongside the extension function (local).
- Option B: route to sibling `PlatformerBuilders.kt` (separated builder file).

**Picked A.** Rationale:
- The new builder has a single consumer (the `tilemapCollision` extension on the same file).
- PATTERNS.md leaves the choice open; CONTEXT.md §D-claude-4 does not constrain.
- The override-tracking builder for per-zone platformerPhysics also lives in `PlatformerExtensions.kt` (line 300 `OverrideTrackingPhysicsBuilder`), so locality is the established convention for builders that are explicitly tied to a specific extension.

The choice can be revisited in Plan 12.1-06 if visitor code needs to share builder helpers — but that would be a refactor, not a structural cost today.
