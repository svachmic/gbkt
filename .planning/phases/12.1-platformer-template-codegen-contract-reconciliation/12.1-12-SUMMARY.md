# Phase 12.1 Plan 12: JVM Emission-Invariant Test for Defect-6 Option-C-Prime Summary

Locked the Plan 12.1-11 Defect-6 fix at the JVM tier with **two new emission-invariant `@Test` methods** in `LevelSwitchEmissionTest`, plus a stale-docstring cleanup at `GBDKPipelineV2.kt:2242`. The new tests prevent a future regression that reverts the `setup_current_level` per-case emission from the literal-bank shape (`_current_area_bank = <N>u;`) back to the unresolvable BANK-macro shape (`_current_area_bank = BANK(_zone_<id>_tilemap);`) — the latter has no SDCC linker producer for data arrays under `#pragma bank N`.
