## Decisions Made

- **Additive evolution via default-arg.** Chose `val enterOps: ... = emptyMap()` over a new visitor method or a refactor of `frameOps` into a generic splice container. Rationale: (a) zero source changes in 5 downstream modules, (b) Plan 10 collector mirrors the existing `frameOps` collector exactly, (c) future genre plugins inherit the same default automatically. Cost: one extra Map field on a data class that already carries two — negligible.
- **Mirror semantics, not a generalization.** Considered `splices: Map<SplicePhase, Map<String, List<ScriptOp>>>` to abstract over enter-vs-frame. Rejected as premature: only 2 phases needed today (enter, frame), and the named-field approach is more discoverable in IDE autocomplete (`result.enterOps` vs `result.splices[SplicePhase.ENTER]`).
- **KDoc cites the future SportVisitor use case explicitly.** The KDoc on `enterOps` names pool spawn / zone tileset / `_camera_target` so a reader who lands on the field at any future date understands the motivating workload without having to git-blame to Plan 11.
