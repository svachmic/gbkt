## NEW gap surfaced — `NAVIGABILITY-PLAYER-CORNER-TRAP`

Reproduction (run against ROM at HEAD `4b7f6a51` + plan 17 merge `20444d7e`):

1. Start fresh: `emulator_start` → step 60 frames → at title screen.
2. Press START → race scene at frame 63.
3. Player at `(80, 80)` (tile 10, 10 — inside polygon).
4. Hold UP 30f → player reaches `(80, 5)`. ✓ traversal works — UP corridor edge.
5. Hold LEFT 25f → player reaches `(5, 80)`. ✓ traversal works — LEFT corridor edge.
6. Hold UP 25f from (80, 80) → player reaches `(5, 80)` (only LEFT moved; UP rejected from x=80).
7. Continue from `(5, 80)`: hold UP+RIGHT 15f → player reaches `(5, 2)`.
8. From `(5, 2)`: hold RIGHT 25f, then DOWN, then RIGHT+DOWN, then UP+RIGHT — **ALL rejected**. `speed_cur` drops to 0. Player frozen at corner.

The player cannot reach checkpoint 1 at pixel `(40, 40)` from spawn via cardinal steering — every cardinal direction's proposed-tile sample at (5, 2) reads wall.

**Hypothesis space:** the polygon-raster + perimeter-walls produces 1-tile-wide drivable strips along corridor edges that admit entry from one axis but reject every exit move because the perpendicular-axis sample is also wall. The waypoint-3x3 force pass widens the corridor at WAYPOINT cells but not BETWEEN waypoints.

**Two candidate remedies (both preserve D-17 / D-09):**

1. **Corridor widening.** Extend the `forceWaypointNeighborhoodsToDrivable` widening along the polygon-edge segments BETWEEN waypoints, OR add a `corridorWidth(tiles = 2)` knob on the `track { }` block.
2. **Corner-aware collision sampling.** Replace single-center-point INT16 sample with 4-corner sample of the proposed sprite hitbox; accept the move if any 1 corner is drivable. Threads narrow corridors without permitting walls.
