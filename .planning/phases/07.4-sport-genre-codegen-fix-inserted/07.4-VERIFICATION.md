## What Plan 18 Changed (Round-3 Delta)

Plan 18 closed the **NAVIGABILITY-PLAYER-CORNER-TRAP** gap identified in the round-2 MCP play-through (Plan 14).

**Root cause confirmed in Plan 14:** The single-center tile-sample at `(propX + spriteW/2, propY + spriteH/2)` rejected every exit move at corner positions where the sprite straddles two wall tiles but has a corner on a drivable strip. Player froze at `(5, 2)` and `(2, 5)` — all four cardinal directions read wall at the center sample point even though the drivable corridor edge was reachable via the sprite's corner.

**Fix delivered in Plan 18:** `SportVisitor.buildFourCornerWallSampleAccept` replaces the single-center sample inside `buildPositionWriteBackWithCollision`. The new accept expression:

```c
if ((NW_corner_ok || NE_corner_ok || SW_corner_ok || SE_corner_ok)) {
    carX = propX; carY = propY;
}
```

A move is accepted iff at least one of the four sprite corners samples a non-WALL tile within map bounds. Solid-wall rejection (all 4 corners on WALL) is preserved, maintaining D-17 (wall impassability).

**Uniformity (D-09):** `buildPositionWriteBackWithCollision` is called from both the player write-back path (main.c line 311) and the AI per-instance write-back path (main.c line 446). Both branches received the 4-corner accept rule automatically.

**JVM-tier lock:** `RacingPlayerTraversabilityTest` (4 `@Test` methods) verifies corner-trap freedom and full-loop player traversability using a pure-Kotlin simulation with `anyCornerDrivable` mirroring the production helper's semantics.

## Lessons Learned (Round 4 — Plan 07.4-24)

### The bug

Round 2 (Plan 14) marked SC-4 (track tilemap visible) as VERIFIED based on
`_current_tileset_id == 1` plus the existence of a high-contrast Plan 17 tileset. The
Plan 14 evidence screenshot at `evidence/racer_14_race_entry_frame129.png` shows a
uniform dark BG with two stacked cars and a "LAP:" HUD — the IDENTICAL state the user
reported in round-4 UAT against the HEAD ROM. The bug was present at round 2; the
verifier did not look at the screenshot pixels.

### The root cause

User-authored `clear()` in `raceScene.enter` lowered to `cls()` and ran AFTER the
racing genre's `set_bkg_tiles` splice (which had paints the BG). `cls()` wiped the
just-painted tilemap. `print("LAP:", ...)` then lowered to BG-layer `gotoxy()/printf()`,
further corrupting the (already-wiped) BG.

Five plans (15-18) built on top of this state. The work was correct; the foundation it
built on was visually broken.

### The fix

- **Plan 07.4-19:** RED tests at the JVM tier locking the contract — the DSL `clear()`
  and `print()` keywords in a scene with a BG splice MUST NOT lower to
  `cls()`/`gotoxy()`/`printf()`. Tests drive the IR via the DSL path (not via
  hand-constructed IR nodes) so they fail at HEAD against the actual racer code path.
- **Plan 07.4-20:** GREEN — TWO-PART fix:
  (a) `ScriptBuilder.clear()` routes to `ScreenClear` IR (one-line gbkt-lang change);
  (b) `visitScreenClear` and `visitPrintOp` become scene-aware in gbkt-backend-gbdk.
  Back-compat for `print()` in non-BG scenes preserved (revised from initial universal
  swap proposal — see Plan 20 SUMMARY).
- **Plan 07.4-21:** Camera-bounds underflow (secondary issue 1) — `kotlin.math.max(0, ...)`
  defensive clamp in `GBDKSystemVisitor.visitCameraSystem`.
- **Plan 07.4-22:** Cross-bank zone-tilemap-access audit (secondary issue 2) — JVM-tier
  audit + conditional `SWITCH_ROM` guard, layered cleanly inside Plan 20's scene-context
  try/finally per its rebase note.
- **Plan 07.4-23:** MCP play-through with VISUAL evidence; UAT-racer.md flipped. Uses
  scope-level grep gates (NOT file-level) so race_enter regressions cannot hide behind
  title_enter back-compat.
- **Plan 07.4-24 (this plan):** Documentation + methodology rule + project-memory
  feedback marker.

### Why the bug took so long to surface
