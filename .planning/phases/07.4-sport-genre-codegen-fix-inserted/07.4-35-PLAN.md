<objective>
Close GAP-TRACK-NOT-RENDERED-AS-CIRCUIT by fixing whichever codegen layer is producing the wrong tilemap shape. The user-runtime UAT reports the rendered tilemap is a static square arena with corner cutouts + checkered interior — NOT a waypoint-driven corridor. Three suspect surfaces:

1. **TrackSynthesizer polygon-rasterization** — the synthesize() function may be filling the polygon interior with drivable tiles (no corridor erosion), or the erosion logic may be off-by-one at the corners.
2. **SportVisitor tile-id mapping** — the synthesizer output values may be correct but the emitter writes them with swapped tile-id mappings (WALL where DRIVABLE was, or vice versa).
3. **Tileset visual values** — the high-contrast Plan 17 tileset values may render a correct corridor in a way that LOOKS like an arena to the human eye (less likely; user said the SHAPE is wrong, not just the contrast).

Diagnostic-first ordering: Task 1 authors DIAGNOSIS.md naming the surface; Task 2 applies the fix to ONLY that surface; Tasks 3+4 verify at JVM and runtime tiers; Task 5 captures cross-checks (existing tests stay GREEN, post-fix ASCII grid matches expected).

- TrackSynthesizerCircuitShapeTest.checkpoint_tiles_are_drivable (CP cells drivable)
