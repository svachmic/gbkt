**Note on hypothesis selection (planner — strong steer, not a hard mandate):**

The pre-plan investigation strongly suggests `H-1: Missing SHOW_BKG`. Pre-plan grep of `main.c` and `bank1.c` at HEAD shows:
- `DISPLAY_ON` and `SHOW_SPRITES` are emitted in `main()` (main.c:514-515)
- `set_bkg_tiles` is emitted in `race_enter` (bank1.c:43)
- `SHOW_BKG` is emitted NOWHERE in the entire codegen (`grep -rn SHOW_BKG gbkt-backend-gbdk/src/main/kotlin/` returns zero hits)

GBDK's `DISPLAY_ON` macro enables LCDC.LCD_ENABLE bit but does NOT enable LCDC.BG_ENABLE. Without the BG enable bit, the tilemap written via `set_bkg_tiles` is never composited to the frame buffer — exactly the "blank screen" symptom. Pong/Breakout/Explorer ROMs ship without rendering BG so the omission has been latent for the entire pre-racer history of the codebase.

The fix is one line in `gbkt-backend-gbdk/src/main/kotlin/io/github/gbkt/backend/gbdk/codegen/pipeline/GBDKPipelineV2.kt` near line 3411 (where `DISPLAY_ON` is emitted in `main()`'s body): add `add(CRawCode("SHOW_BKG;"))` after the `SHOW_SPRITES` line.

**However**, the executor MUST defer to whatever Plan 25's DIAGNOSIS.md recommends. If DIAGNOSIS.md names a different hypothesis (e.g. camera clamp degenerate, GBC palette black-on-black), follow DIAGNOSIS.md — the planner's strong steer is informational, not authoritative.
