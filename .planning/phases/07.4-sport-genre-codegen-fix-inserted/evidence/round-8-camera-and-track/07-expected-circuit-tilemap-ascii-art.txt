#   D-17  (enclosed loop, no cutting)
#     The polygon interior (rows 7-13 x cols 7-13) is NOT drivable — it is
#     grass (or wall). If interior cells were drivable, the player could cut

#     "A lap means a real lap."

#     gates the player must drive across. Both lie inside the 3-tile-wide
#     corridor band by construction.
