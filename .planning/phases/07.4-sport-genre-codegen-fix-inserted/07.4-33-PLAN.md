<objective>
Lock GAP-TRACK-NOT-RENDERED-AS-CIRCUIT at the JVM rasterization tier AND the runtime tile-array tier via two RED tests that FAIL against HEAD. These tests become the GREEN contract Plan 07.4-35 must satisfy.

The round-7 false-positive class: SC-4-VISUAL passed on pixel-signature thresholds (unique_pixels >= 4, non_bg_pixels >= 2000). The user's manual UAT then showed the rendered tilemap is a static square arena with corner cutouts + checkered interior — NOT the waypoint-driven road corridor the `racing("track1") { waypoint(5,5,cp) waypoint(15,5) waypoint(15,15,cp) waypoint(5,15) }` DSL declares. The structural lesson (D-N-12, codified in this plan and Plan 07.4-32): visual SCs whose verdict depends on tilemap SHAPE require per-tile structural assertions, not pixel-count thresholds.

This plan codifies D-N-12 for tilemap shape. The expected-shape derivation is hand-encoded as ASCII art in evidence and inline in the test source — reviewer can visually confirm the GREEN contract. Plan 07.4-35 will fix the synthesizer (or downstream tile-value mapping) until the rendered tilemap matches the expected corridor at every cell.

The suspect surfaces named in the gap inventory:
1. TrackSynthesizer polygon-rasterization output may produce arena-shape rather than corridor (the corridor erosion / scanline fill is off)
2. _zone_track1_tiles[] values may be correct but the tile-id-to-pixel mapping (high-contrast Plan 17 tileset) makes a correct corridor LOOK like an arena
3. set_bkg_tiles HOME-bank helper (Plan 30) may be rendering the wrong tilemap (e.g. byte-order, offset, or wrong array)

These tests do not pre-judge which surface is the fix surface. They lock the CONTRACT: synthesizer output AND runtime tile-array AND rendered tilemap must match the expected corridor. Plan 07.4-35 is responsible for diagnosing and fixing whichever layer is wrong.
</objective>
