### ROM size + bank distribution

- `racer.gb`: 65,536 bytes (64 KB).
- Bank distribution (from `racer.noi`):
  - HOME (`l__CODE`): 2,821 bytes (0xB05).
  - Bank 1 (`l__CODE_1`): 353 bytes (0x161) — banked scene code.
  - Bank 2 (`l__CODE_2`): 361 bytes (0x169) — zone tilemap data (D-12 synthesized track tiles).
  - Cartridge upgraded ROM_ONLY → MBC5 (banking detected); MBC type 0x19.
