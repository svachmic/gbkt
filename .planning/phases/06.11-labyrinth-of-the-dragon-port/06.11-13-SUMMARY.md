## Decisions Made
- **WorldFlags has 11 FlagRefs** (not just 8 boss flags): floors 2-4 have both boss AND elite defeat tracked separately, matching Original's ABILITY_N grants and story progression model
- **showMessage/grantItem replaced with callOp()**: The DSL methods `showMessage()` and `grantItem()` documented in older CLAUDE.md examples do not exist in ScriptBuilder — correct approach is `callOp("map_textbox", StringLiteral("str_key"))` and `callOp("add_item", StringLiteral("item_id"))`
- **Flag page capacity**: A single `page()` block supports up to 8 named flags — the world flags page uses 11 flags total (11 defeat states) which required verifying DSL page capacity
