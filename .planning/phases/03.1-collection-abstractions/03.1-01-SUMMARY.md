## Decisions Made

- `IRColl` prefix used to avoid collision with existing entity Pool IR (`IRPoolSpawn`, `IRPoolUpdate`, etc.)
- `collPools` field name in Game (not `pools`) to avoid shadowing existing `pools: List<Pool>` entity field
- GameScope uses `open` methods with no-op defaults; GameBuilder overrides — consistent with existing `registerVariable`/`registerArray` pattern
- SimulationContext updated with not-simulated stubs (collection operations are hardware-backed, not simulatable in pure JVM)
